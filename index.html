About

Engineering that survives contact with real users

I build the unglamorous parts properly: migrations, permissions, billing, retries, and logs.

I am a full-stack developer and AI automation engineer working remotely with teams in Germany, the United States, and Malaysia. My day splits between shipping client-facing product work and wiring the automation that removes manual steps behind it.

Most of my recent work is multi-tenant: shared codebase, isolated data, role-aware dashboards. I care about the boring guarantees — that a split bill reconciles, a webhook retries, and a tenant can never read another tenant's rows.
